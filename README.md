## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Engineer** (Batman University) building end-to-end software — from AI backends and cloud infrastructure to mobile apps and robotic systems. I enjoy taking an idea all the way to a product that real users touch.

- 🌙 **Founder & developer of _Nur Çeşmesi_** — an AI-powered mobile Q&A platform built on a RAG architecture, live on cloud infrastructure 24/7
- 🤖 **TÜBİTAK 2209-A R&D project** — voice-interactive physical information robot (Raspberry Pi 5 + ESP32)
- 🦾 **Robotics simulation** — vision-guided UR5e pick-and-place in Webots; ABB YuMi dual-arm modelling in MATLAB Simulink/Simscape
- 🏛 **Team project** — database-driven municipal service & complaint management portal (Node.js + Docker)
- 📫 Reach me at **furknaylmz@gmail.com**
