## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Engineer** and **Mobile Developer** building cross-platform apps for **Android & iOS** with **Flutter** and **Dart** — from clean, modern UI to local data, authentication and AI-powered backends. I enjoy taking an app from idea all the way to the store.

- 🌙 **Founder & developer of _Nur Çeşmesi_** — an AI-powered mobile app running on Android & iOS, with a RAG backend live 24/7 in the cloud
- 🎬 **_Sinevo_** — a cinematic movie discovery app with a dark, premium design
- 🧠 **_QueazyApp_** — a vocabulary learning app with custom lists, quizzes and SQLite storage
- 🎓 Computer Engineer — Batman University
- 📫 Reach me at **furknaylmz@gmail.com**
