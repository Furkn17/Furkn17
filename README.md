## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Engineer** (Batman University) building end-to-end software — from AI backends and cloud infrastructure to cross-platform mobile apps and robotic systems. I enjoy taking an idea all the way to a product that real users touch.

- 🌙 **Founder & developer of _Nur Çeşmesi_** — an AI-powered mobile Q&A platform built on a RAG architecture, live on cloud infrastructure 24/7
- 📱 **Flutter apps** — _Sinevo_, a cinematic movie discovery app, and _QueazyApp_, a vocabulary learning app with SQLite
- 🦾 **Robotics simulation** — robot arm pick-and-place in Webots and MATLAB Simulink
- 🏛 **Team project** — database-driven municipal service & complaint management portal (Node.js + Docker)
- 📫 Reach me at **furknaylmz@gmail.com**
